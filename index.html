Xiaowen Qiu

PhD Student in CS, UMass

I am a first-year PhD student in CS at UMass Amherst, advised by Prof. Chuang Gan. My research focuses on robot learning, with the goal of building general-purpose robots that can learn diverse skills and adapt to unfamiliar tasks and environments. Prior to my PhD, I was a full-time research scientist at Genesis AI. I received my master’s degree from UMass Amherst and my bachelor’s degree from Fudan University.
